Retail specialists

Deterrence, observation and customer awareness.

Our retail security personnel are briefed to support the client’s store procedures while maintaining a professional and approachable presence. Their role can include monitoring entrances and exits, observing suspicious behaviour, supporting loss-prevention procedures, checking receipts where this forms part of the client’s authorised process, and responding appropriately to incidents.
